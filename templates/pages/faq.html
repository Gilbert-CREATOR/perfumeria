Frequently Asked Questions

What makes D.A.R.C.Y. different?

We focus on minimalist design principles in perfumery, creating scents that are simple yet sophisticated, removing the unnecessary to reveal the true essence of each fragrance.

How long do your fragrances last?

Our fragrances typically last 6-8 hours on skin and up to 24 hours on clothing. Longevity varies depending on the specific scent and your body chemistry.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes, we are committed to ethical practices. None of our products are tested on animals, and we use only cruelty-free ingredients.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused products in their original packaging. Please contact our customer service team to initiate a return.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. International orders typically arrive within 7-14 business days.

How should I store my fragrances?

Store your fragrances in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Avoid storing them in bathrooms as humidity can affect the scent quality.

Can I create a custom fragrance?

Yes, we offer custom fragrance creation services. Contact our studio to schedule a consultation with our perfumers to create your unique signature scent.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and various digital payment methods. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our customer service team is here to help.
